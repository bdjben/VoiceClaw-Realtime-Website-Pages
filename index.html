Frequently asked questions

Open Support
What are the defaults for a fresh setup?

Fresh iPhone and Apple Watch setups use GPT Live for voice and GPT-6.1 Sol in GPT Direct for tasks. You can choose another model or agent. Updating preserves your saved voice engine and route selections.

Can I use VoiceClaw Realtime without a computer?

Yes. Talk with GPT Live or GPT Realtime on iPhone or Apple Watch without a desktop Companion. Use GPT Direct for deeper intelligence and live web search with GPT-6, GPT-6.1, and GPT-6.2 models, plus local actions on iPhone. Add Companion only for Codex, OpenClaw, and Hermes on your computer.

Can I choose GPT-6.2 Astra or GPT-6.2 Sol?

Yes. Choose GPT-6.2 Astra or GPT-6.2 Sol in GPT Direct, alongside GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6 Astra, and ChatGPT Instant.

Which GPT Direct models can I choose?

The model order is GPT-6.2 Astra, GPT-6.2 Sol, GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6.1 Terra, GPT-6 Astra, then ChatGPT Instant. This Direct lineup does not alter models configured inside your own OpenClaw, Hermes, or Codex agents.

Can I switch routes without ending the voice session?

Yes. Switch among Direct models, Codex, OpenClaw, and Hermes, or send a one-off task to another route while your voice conversation continues. VoiceClaw Realtime keeps the voice engine and task route separate.

What does the Companion do?

The free Companion connects VoiceClaw Realtime to Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes, files, and computer control on your Mac or PC. On Mac, scan the Companion QR on iPhone, then sync Apple Watch settings for automatic Companion pairing and connection. Tailscale and a personal HTTPS tunnel are optional.

Does Apple Watch work away from my iPhone?

Yes. Use direct GPT Live, GPT Realtime, or GPT Direct over Watch Wi-Fi or cellular without a desktop Companion. For Mac computer-side agents, pair on iPhone and sync Apple Watch settings first; the Watch can then connect to Companion independently. Optionally, connect via your personal Tailscale network or personal non-Tailscale HTTPS tunnel. Whether you use the built-in device pairing or your own Tailscale credentials or tunnel, your connection to your computer for OpenClaw / Codex / Hermes tasks is encrypted. With direct sign-in, your GPT Live / GPT Realtime and GPT-6/6.1/6.2 conversations connect directly to OpenAI through your signed-in ChatGPT account.

Where are my credentials stored?

Credentials added on iPhone are stored in the device Keychain. Companion stores its own settings on your computer. Live voice and GPT Direct requests connect to your AI provider; computer requests go to your paired Companion. Automatic connection services carry encrypted device traffic and process connection metadata. See the Privacy Policy for these separate data flows.

Does VoiceClaw Realtime require a separate subscription?

VoiceClaw Realtime is free. You can sign in with your existing ChatGPT subscription for supported routes.
