Open Support
Can I use VoiceClaw Realtime without a computer?

Yes. Talk with GPT Realtime or GPT Live, use GPT-5.6 Direct, search the web, analyze files, and use supported iPhone actions without Companion. Install Companion only when you want to reach Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes, computer files, or computer control.

Can I switch routes without ending the voice session?

Yes. GPT Realtime or GPT Live keeps the conversation going while you switch among ChatGPT Instant, GPT-5.6 Direct, Codex, OpenClaw, and Hermes. You can also send a one-off task through another route without leaving your current one.

What does the Companion do?

The free Companion connects VoiceClaw Realtime on iPhone and Apple Watch to Codex, OpenClaw, Hermes, files, and computer control on your Mac or PC. Pair it by scanning one QR code.

Does Apple Watch work away from my iPhone?

Yes. GPT Realtime, GPT Live, and GPT-5.6 Direct can use the Watch's Wi-Fi or cellular connection without a personal HTTPS tunnel. To reach Codex, OpenClaw, or Hermes when your iPhone is not nearby, configure your own personal HTTPS tunnel to Companion using the Apple Watch setup guide.

Where are my credentials stored?

Credentials added on iPhone are stored in the device Keychain. Companion stores its own settings on your computer. VoiceClaw Realtime does not operate a developer server that receives your conversations or credentials.

Does VoiceClaw Realtime require a separate subscription?

VoiceClaw Realtime is free. You can sign in with your existing ChatGPT subscription for supported routes. ChatGPT Instant currently requires your own OpenAI API key. Companion is also free.
