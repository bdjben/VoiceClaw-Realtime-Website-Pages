VoiceClaw Realtime

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

VoiceClaw Realtime is an iPhone and Apple Watch app for realtime AI voice interactions with supported OpenAI voice and reasoning models, plus optional Codex, OpenClaw, and Hermes access through VoiceClaw Companion on a user's computer. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information you provide

You may sign in with ChatGPT or enter an OpenAI API key, computer connection details, optional gateway credentials, preferred model settings, and VoiceClaw Companion pairing data. Pairing also creates device identities and keys for authenticated connections. These settings are used to operate the features you configure.

Credentials and local storage

Credentials entered on iPhone are stored in the device Keychain. Synced Watch credentials and connection settings are stored on the Watch. VoiceClaw Companion stores its own configuration and device keys locally on the computer. Users keep their own AI accounts and agent installations, and may choose their own optional network routes.

Audio and voice interactions

When you start GPT Live or GPT Realtime, microphone audio and conversation data are sent to the AI provider for processing. Direct voice can connect from iPhone or Apple Watch without a desktop Companion. If you select a Companion-relayed voice route, traffic also passes through your computer. Voice responses play through the output selected by your device and audio settings.

OpenAI and AI services

VoiceClaw uses OpenAI services for GPT Live, GPT Realtime, GPT Direct, live web search, transcription, text-to-speech, and related AI functionality when you use those features. Your requests and any content you choose to attach are sent to the selected AI service. Information sent to OpenAI is handled by OpenAI according to OpenAI's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Computer agents and automatic connections

Computer-agent requests and returned files pass between your device and your paired Companion, then to the Codex, OpenClaw, or Hermes installation you select. Those agents may contact their own configured AI services and tools. They keep their own accounts and configuration.

Automatic Mac Companion pairing uses connection services to help paired devices find and reach one another. Those services can carry encrypted device traffic and process connection metadata such as public device identifiers and network addresses. The connection is separate from the direct live-voice connection to your AI provider. VoiceClaw also contacts update and download services when checking for or retrieving releases.

Optional Tailscale and HTTPS routes

Tailscale and a personal HTTPS tunnel are optional alternatives to automatic Mac pairing. If you choose them, requests pass through the network route you configure. Tailscale and any tunnel provider may process traffic and network metadata according to their own service behavior and policies.

Device permissions

VoiceClaw may request access to the microphone for live voice, camera for setup QR scanning, photo library for user-selected media analysis, contacts, calendar, reminders, and location only when relevant app features are used or explicitly requested. The app does not silently read contacts, photos, messages, WhatsApp, notes, or other app data.

Subscriptions and purchases

VoiceClaw subscriptions are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Apple processes payment information. VoiceClaw receives subscription entitlement information needed to determine whether app access is active.

Diagnostics and support

The app includes diagnostics to help users confirm that audio, credentials, OpenClaw, Companion, Watch sync, and network routes are working. If you contact support, you may choose to share screenshots, recordings, diagnostic text, or other information needed to resolve the issue.

Children

VoiceClaw is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as VoiceClaw changes. The updated version will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@voiceclawrealtime.com.
